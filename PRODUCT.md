# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, employers, and creative collaborators who arrive via referral, social, or direct search. They're evaluating Christian's taste and craft before reaching out. Context: usually a laptop or phone, often quickly scanning before deciding whether to dig in.

## Product Purpose

Christian Dizon's portfolio — a body of work that shows what he builds and how he thinks. Success means a visitor goes from "who is this?" to "I need to work with them" within one scroll session. The playground section specifically houses creative experiments: landing pages, interactive experiments, things built during creative bursts.

## Brand Personality

Playful, sharp, restless. Work that has fun with craft without losing its edge. Always moving, always trying something new, but every choice is intentional.

## Anti-references

- Generic Framer agency templates: smooth scroll + gradient blob + floating card grids
- Dribbble-safe minimal: pastel, rounded, no point of view
- Dev portfolio defaults: GitHub green, tech stack badges as decoration

## Design Principles

1. **Show the thinking, not just the output.** Every project and experiment should communicate the "why" behind the move, not just the final artifact.
2. **Playfulness earns its place.** Fun is intentional — it comes from a specific idea, not decoration for its own sake.
3. **Restless means never settling.** Each section or experiment should feel like it came from someone who's already thinking about what comes next.
4. **Sharp over safe.** Strong opinions rendered in code. Nothing apologetic.
5. **Craft is the argument.** The portfolio itself is the proof. Polish, motion, and detail are the pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Sufficient contrast on all text. Keyboard navigable.
